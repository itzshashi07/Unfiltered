## d15 — INDIAN SEXUAL HEALTH MYTHS & CULTURAL REALITIES

Investigate common misconceptions, including:
* Does masturbation cause weakness?
* Does masturbation cause infertility?
* Are wet dreams dangerous?
* Can periods make someone impure?
* Can someone exercise during menstruation?
* Can someone's sexual history be determined from the hymen?
* Is physical appearance evidence of sexual experience?
* Does marriage automatically establish sexual consent?
* Can HIV spread through casual contact?
* Can pregnancy occur the first time someone has sex?
* Are all STIs visibly symptomatic?
* Can emergency contraception terminate an established pregnancy?
* Do all bodies develop at the same speed?
* Can social media provide reliable medical advice?
* Are harmful relationship behaviors evidence of love?
Each myth card needs:
1. The original claim
2. A verdict
3. A factual explanation
4. Why people believe it
5. A credible reference
6. A related lesson
Use thoughtful language and avoid shaming cultural communities.

* Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita provisions relevant to sexual offences
* Cybercrime reporting
* Image-based sexual abuse

* Medical Termination of Pregnancy legislation and rules
* Minor consent and guardian requirements where applicable
* Medical confidentiality and its limits
* Support services and legal assistance
* School responsibilities
* Help-seeking pathways
* Student safety rights

Every legal lesson must show:
* Jurisdiction
* Relevant law
* Plain-language explanation
* Official reference URL
* Last verification date
* Legal review status
* Warning that laws can change
