## Full description (max 4000)
UNFILTERED is a sexual health and relationships education app for adults in India, in English, Hindi and Hinglish. It explains the things people wonder about but rarely get clear answers to, in plain, judgement-free language.

WHAT'S INSIDE (18+ EDITION)
• 46 lessons across 18 topics: the body, sexual health, periods, contraception, pregnancy and fertility, STIs, consent, relationships, gender and identity, online safety, porn and media literacy, mental health, Indian law and rights, and life skills
• 116 myth cards that check common beliefs against the evidence, each with its sources
• 130 prepared answers to real questions, searchable offline in Ask Unfiltered (it is not an AI chatbot)
• Interactive labs: Body Lab, Period Lab, Protection Lab, STI Challenge, Consent Simulator, Relationship Radar, Digital Safety Lab and more
• Quizzes and decision-path scenarios for practising hard moments and conversations
• Optional "Topics I want to explore" filters: men, women, transgender and non-binary people, intersex variations and body topics. They only organise content; they never ask who you are
• Illustrations and an at-a-glance visual summary in every lesson. Some pictures are AI-generated and labelled as such in the app
• Safety Center with Indian helplines, and a References Center listing every source

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
• Works fully offline; the app does not request internet access
• Your progress and notes stay on your phone, and you can delete everything in Settings

IMPORTANT
UNFILTERED is for education only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and a lawyer for legal advice. In an emergency in India, call 112.

The content was drafted with AI assistance from the public-health and legal sources listed in the app's References Center. It has not yet been reviewed by a qualified medical, legal or child-safeguarding professional.

This edition is for adults aged 18 and over.
